Private runtime font dependencies (not vendored in the source tree)

scripts/bootstrap_fonts.py downloads unmodified Noto Sans, Noto Sans Arabic,
Noto Sans Hebrew (Regular/Bold) from https://github.com/notofonts/noto-fonts
revision ffebf8c1ee449e544955a7e813c54f9b73848eac, and monochrome Noto Emoji
from https://github.com/google/fonts revision e7db6ca603fd4862010383ff17ffd6cc4ffac1c0.
Every font and original license file is SHA-256 checked by the bootstrap.
These fonts are licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1.
The original LICENSE and NotoEmoji-OFL.txt are installed beside the fonts in
.local/share/shiraz/fonts and must accompany redistribution of those fonts.
No font binaries are committed and no host font configuration is modified.
