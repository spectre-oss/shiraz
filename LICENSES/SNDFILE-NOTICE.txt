Local MediaPlayer audio decoding dynamically links to the host libsndfile library.
Project and source: https://libsndfile.github.io/libsndfile/
License: LGPL-2.1-or-later. No libsndfile source is vendored in Shiraz.
Building the playback bridge requires the libsndfile development headers and library.
AppImage dependency scanning bundles the shared library and its dependencies and
records their installed package versions, license identifiers, and hashes in the
bundle metadata. The library remains independently licensed and replaceable.
