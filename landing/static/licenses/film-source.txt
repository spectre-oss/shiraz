Shiraz Launcher film (media/shiraz-launcher.mp4, media/shiraz-launcher.jpg)

The film and its poster are not in the source tree. The site build downloads
them from the project release named in src/lib/media.json and checks each file
against the size and SHA-256 recorded there.

Picture: screen recordings of a Shiraz development build on an Apple Silicon Mac,
with title cards made for the film.

Sound: synthetic narration, with music and sound effects.

Third-party apps: the recordings show Android apps and the F-Droid catalog. Apps
shown running or listed in the launcher include 2048 Open Fun Game, SolitaireCG,
Dodge, Editor, F-Droid, OsmAnd~ and Telegram. Their names, icons and interfaces
belong to their own projects and keep their own licenses. They appear as
recorded. They are not part of Shiraz and are not covered by its GPL-3.0-only
license. An app listed in the launcher or the catalog is not thereby known to run.
